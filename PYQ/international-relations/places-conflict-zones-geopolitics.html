  1. 1 2023

    Consider the following statements:

    Statement-I: Israel has established diplomatic relations with some Arab States.
    Statement-II: The ‘Arab Peace Initiative’ mediated by Saudi Arabia was signed by Israel and Arab League.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

    1. ABoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
    2. BBoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
    3. CStatement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
    4. DStatement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

    Explanation

    Statement-I is correct: Israel has established diplomatic relations with several Arab states, including Egypt (1979), Jordan (1994), and, via the 2020 Abraham Accords, the UAE, Bahrain, Sudan, and Morocco. Statement-II is incorrect: the Arab Peace Initiative, proposed by Saudi Arabia in 2002 and endorsed by the Arab League, was never signed by Israel.

  2. 2 2024

    Consider the following statements:

    Statement-I: There is instability and a worsening security situation in the Sahel region.
    Statement-II: There have been military takeovers/coups d'état in several countries of the Sahel region in the recent past.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

    1. ABoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I
    2. BBoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct, but Statement-II does not explain Statement-I
    3. CStatement-I is correct, but Statement-II is incorrect.
    4. DStatement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct.
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I

    Explanation

    The Sahel region, including Niger, has seen political instability, with Niger's military taking power in 2023. Both statements are correct and Statement-II directly explains Statement-I.

  3. 3 2024

    Consider the following pairs:

    Country : Reason for being in the news
    1. Argentina : Worst economic crisis
    2. Sudan : War between the country's regular army and paramilitary forces
    3. Turkey : Rescinded its membership of NATO

    How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

    1. AOnly one pair
    2. BOnly two pairs
    3. CAll three pairs
    4. DNone of the pairs
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Only two pairs

    Explanation

    Argentina and Sudan face crises. Turkey has not rescinded NATO membership. Only two pairs (Argentina and Sudan) are correctly matched.

  4. 4 2024

    Consider the following statements:

    Statement-I: Sumed pipeline is a strategic route for Persian Gulf oil and natural gas shipments to Europe.
    Statement-II: Sumed pipeline connects the Red Sea with the Mediterranean Sea.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

    1. ABoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I
    2. BBoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct, but Statement-II does not explain Statement-I
    3. CStatement-I is correct, but Statement-II is incorrect.
    4. DStatement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct.
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I

    Explanation

    The Sumed pipeline connects the Red Sea to the Mediterranean, bypassing the Suez Canal. Both statements are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I.

  5. 5 2024

    Consider the following countries:

    1. Italy
    2. Japan
    3. Nigeria
    4. South Korea
    5. South Africa

    Which of the above countries are frequently mentioned in the media for their low birth rates, aging population or declining population?

    1. A1, 2 and 4
    2. B1, 3 and 5
    3. C2 and 4 only
    4. D3 and 5 only
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. 1, 2 and 4

    Explanation

    Italy and Japan's fertility rates are at record lows. Nigeria's growth rate is 2.41%, and South Korea's rate is the world's lowest. Countries 1 (Italy), 2 (Japan), and 4 (South Korea) are frequently mentioned for declining populations.

  6. 6 2023

    Consider the following pairs:

    Regions often mentioned in news : Reason for being in news
    1. North Kivu and Ituri : War between Armenia and Azerbaijan
    2. Nagorno-Karabakh : Insurgency in Mozambique
    3. Kherson and Zaporizhzhia : Dispute between Israel and Lebanon

    How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

    1. AOnly one
    2. BOnly two
    3. CAll three
    4. DNone
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. None

    Explanation

    North Kivu & Ituri (DRC), and Nagorno-Karabakh (Armenia and Azerbaijan) face conflicts, while Kherson and Zaporizhzhia are unrelated to Israel-Lebanon disputes. All three pairs are incorrectly matched — Answer is None.

  8. 8 2023

    In the recent years Chad, Guinea, Mali and Sudan caught international attention for which one of the following reasons is common to all of them?

    1. ADiscovery of rich deposits of rare earth elements
    2. BEstablishment of Chinese military bases
    3. CSouthward expansion of Sahara Desert
    4. DSuccessful coups
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Successful coups

    Explanation

    Chad, Guinea, Mali, and Sudan all experienced military takeovers (Successful Coups) in recent years.

  9. 9 2022

    Which one of the following statements best reflects the issue with Senkaku Islands, sometimes mentioned in the news?

    1. AIt is generally believed that they are artificial islands made by a country around the South China Sea.
    2. BChina and Japan engage in maritime disputes over these islands in the East China Sea.
    3. CA permanent American military base has been set up there to help Taiwan to increase its defence capabilities.
    4. DThough the International Court of Justice declared them as no man's land, some South-East Asian countries claim them.
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. China and Japan engage in maritime disputes over these islands in the East China Sea.

    Explanation

    The Senkaku Islands in the East China Sea are disputed between Japan, China, and Taiwan.

  10. 10 2022

    Which one of the following statements best describes the 'Polar Code'?

    1. AIt is the international code of safety for ships operating in polar waters.
    2. BIt is the agreement of the countries around the North Pole regarding the demarcation of their territories in the polar region.
    3. CIt is a set of norms to be followed by the countries whose scientists undertake research studies in the North Pole and South Pole.
    4. DIt is a trade and security agreement of the member countries of the Arctic Council.
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. It is the international code of safety for ships operating in polar waters.

    Explanation

    The International Maritime Organization's Polar Code ensures safe operations and environmental protection in polar regions and provides for safe ship operation and environmental protection.

  11. 11 2022

    Consider the following pairs:

    Country : Important reason for being in the news recently
    1. Chad : Setting up of permanent military base by China
    2. Guinea : Suspension of Constitution and Government by military
    3. Lebanon : Severe and prolonged economic depression
    4. Tunisia : Suspension of Parliament by President

    How many pairs given above are correctly matched?

    1. AOnly one pair
    2. BOnly two pairs
    3. COnly three pairs
    4. DAll four pairs
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Only three pairs

    Explanation

    China's only overseas military base is in Djibouti, not Equatorial Guinea. Other crises include Guinea's coup, Lebanon's crisis, and Tunisia's suspension of parliament. Pairs 2, 3, and 4 are correctly matched — three pairs.

  13. 13 2024

    Consider the following statements:

    Statement-I: Recently, Venezuela has achieved a rapid recovery from its economic crisis and succeeded in preventing its people from fleeing/emigrating to other countries.
    Statement-II: Venezuela has the world's largest oil reserves.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

    1. ABoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I
    2. BBoth Statement-I and Statement-II are correct, but Statement-II does not explain Statement-I
    3. CStatement-I is correct, but Statement-II is incorrect
    4. DStatement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Statement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct

    Explanation

    Statement-I is incorrect: Venezuela remains in a prolonged economic crisis with poverty, hyperinflation, and continued mass emigration (over 7.7 million have fled). Statement-II is correct: Venezuela holds the world's largest proven oil reserves (over 300 billion barrels), ahead of Saudi Arabia.

  14. 14 2021

    Consider the following statements in respect of the ICC World Test Championship:

    1. The finalists were decided by the number of matches they won.
    2. New Zealand was ranked ahead of England because it won more matches than England.

    Which of the above statements is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Neither 1 nor 2

    Explanation

    Both statements are incorrect. Rankings were based on the Points Percentage System (PCT), not simply the number of wins. England won more matches than New Zealand in the WTC cycle, but New Zealand had a higher PCT and secured the final berth on that basis.

  15. 15 2022

    Consider the following pairs:

    Country : Region often mentioned in the news
    1. Anatolia : Turkey
    2. Amhara : Ethiopia
    3. Cabo Delgado : Spain
    4. Catalonia : Italy

    How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

    1. AOnly one pair
    2. BOnly two pairs
    3. COnly three pairs
    4. DAll four pairs
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Only two pairs

    Explanation

    Pair 1 is correctly matched: Anatolia is a historical region covering most of modern-day Türkiye. Pair 2 is correctly matched: Amhara is a region in Ethiopia in the news due to conflicts involving the Ethiopian government and Tigray forces. Pair 3 is incorrectly matched: Cabo Delgado is in Mozambique, not Spain. Pair 4 is incorrectly matched: Catalonia is in Spain, not Italy. Only two pairs are correctly matched.

  16. 16 2022

    The term ‘Levant’, often heard in the news, roughly corresponds to which of the following regions?

    1. ARegion along the eastern Mediterranean shores
    2. BRegion along North African shores stretching from Egypt to Morocco
    3. CRegion along the Persian Gulf and Horn of Africa
    4. DThe entire coastal areas of the Mediterranean Sea
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Region along the eastern Mediterranean shores

    Explanation

    The Levant refers to the eastern Mediterranean region covering Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Israel, Palestine, and parts of Turkey and Egypt (Sinai Peninsula). The region along North Africa from Egypt to Morocco is called the Maghreb, and the term Levant does not cover the entire Mediterranean coastline.

  17. 17 2023

    Which one of the following countries has been suffering from decades of civil strife and food shortages and was in news in the recent past for its very severe famine?

    1. AAngola
    2. BCosta Rica
    3. CEcuador
    4. DSomalia
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Somalia

    Explanation

    Somalia has endured decades of civil strife and political instability since its central government collapsed in 1991. Recurring droughts, exacerbated by climate change and conflict, caused severe food shortages and famine, most acutely in 2022–23.

  18. 18 2015

    The area known as ‘Golan Heights’ sometimes appears in the news in the context of the events related to:

    1. ACentral Asia
    2. BMiddle East
    3. CSouth-East Asia
    4. DCentral Africa
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Middle East

    Explanation

    Golan Heights is a region in the Middle East, contested between Israel and Syria; Israel has controlled most of the territory since the 1967 Arab-Israeli War.

  19. 19 2015

    Among the following, which were frequently mentioned in the news for the outbreak of Ebola virus recently?

    1. ASyria and Jordan
    2. BGuinea, Sierra Leone and Liberia
    3. CPhilippines and Papua New Guinea
    4. DJamaica, Haiti and Surinam
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Guinea, Sierra Leone and Liberia

    Explanation

    The 2014–2016 Ebola outbreak devastated West Africa, with Guinea, Sierra Leone, and Liberia at its epicentre — the largest Ebola epidemic ever recorded at the time.

  20. 20 2015

    Recently, a series of uprisings of people referred to as ‘Arab Spring’ originally started from:

    1. AEgypt
    2. BLebanon
    3. CSyria
    4. DTunisia
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Tunisia

    Explanation

    The Arab Spring began in Tunisia in December 2010, triggered by the self-immolation of street vendor Mohamed Bouazizi in Sidi Bouzid, sparking protests that led to the fall of President Ben Ali in January 2011 and inspired similar uprisings across the Arab world.

  21. 21 2018

    In which one of the following countries have lakhs of people either suffered from severe famine/acute malnutrition or died due to starvation caused by war/ethnic conflicts (very recently)?

    1. AAngola and Zambia
    2. BMorocco and Tunisia
    3. CVenezuela and Colombia
    4. DYemen and South Sudan
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Yemen and South Sudan

    Explanation

    Yemen and South Sudan have experienced severe humanitarian crises, with millions suffering famine and acute malnutrition primarily due to ongoing wars and ethnic conflicts — Yemen's civil war since 2015 and South Sudan's civil war since 2013.

  22. 22 2022

    Consider the following statements:

    1. Bidibidi is a large refugee settlement in north-western Kenya.
    2. Some people who fled from South Sudan civil war live in Bidibidi.
    3. Some people who fled from civil war in Somalia live in Dadaab refugee complex in Kenya.

    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 and 2
    2. B2 only
    3. C2 and 3
    4. D3 only
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. 2 and 3

    Explanation

    Statement 1 is incorrect: Bidibidi is one of the world's largest refugee settlements, located in north-western Uganda, not Kenya. Statement 2 is correct: it shelters people displaced by the South Sudan civil war (2013–2018). Statement 3 is correct: the Dadaab refugee complex in north-eastern Kenya has sheltered Somali refugees since the 1991 outbreak of civil war in Somalia.
