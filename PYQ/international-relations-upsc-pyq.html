Frequently asked questions

How many questions come from International Relations in UPSC Prelims?

International Relations typically accounts for 8-12 questions in UPSC Prelims GS Paper 1 each year, spanning international organisations, regional blocs, conflict zones and India's bilateral ties — making it one of the highest-yield current-affairs-linked areas.

Which International Relations topics are most important for UPSC Prelims?

Economic & Trade Groupings (G20, BRICS, WTO, RCEP) and Places, Conflict Zones & Current Geopolitics have produced the most questions historically, followed closely by Security, Nuclear & Regional Blocs.

Is NCERT enough to solve these International Relations PYQs?

Not on its own. Unlike Ancient History, International Relations is almost entirely current-affairs-driven — NCERT gives you the conceptual base (types of organisations, treaties, terms), but you need a daily newspaper or a monthly current-affairs digest to cover the actual summits, organisations and conflict zones UPSC tests.
