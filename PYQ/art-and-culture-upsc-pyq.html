Frequently asked questions

How many questions come from Art and Culture in UPSC Prelims?

Art and Culture typically accounts for 4-8 questions in UPSC Prelims GS Paper 1 each year, drawing heavily on Architecture, Performing Arts, and current-affairs-linked topics like GI tags and UNESCO heritage sites.

Which Art and Culture topics are most important for UPSC Prelims?

Architecture and Sculpture, Religious and Scientific Literature, Performing Arts, and Philosophy, Bhakti & Sufi Movements have produced the most questions historically, with GI Tags and crafts gaining weight in recent years due to current affairs linkages.

Is NCERT enough to solve these Art and Culture PYQs?

NCERT Fine Arts textbooks and Nitin Singhania's "Indian Art and Culture" cover most concepts, but many recent questions are current-affairs driven — new GI tags, UNESCO inscriptions, and festivals in the news — so pairing static material with a current affairs source is essential.
