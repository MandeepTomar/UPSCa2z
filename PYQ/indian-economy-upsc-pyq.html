Frequently asked

How many questions come from Indian Economy in UPSC Prelims?

Indian Economy typically accounts for 15-18 questions in UPSC Prelims GS Paper 1 each year, making it one of the highest-scoring dynamic subjects when prepared topic-wise.

Which Indian Economy topics are most important for UPSC Prelims?

Infrastructure & Industry, Human Development & Poverty, Agriculture & Rural Development, and the External Sector have produced the most questions historically, followed by Banking and Fiscal Policy.

Is a standard textbook enough to solve these PYQs, or do I need current affairs too?

A base like Ramesh Singh or Sriram's notes covers most static concepts, but Indian Economy PYQs increasingly blend static concepts with the latest Budget, Economic Survey, and RBI policy — so current affairs revision is essential alongside these PYQs.
