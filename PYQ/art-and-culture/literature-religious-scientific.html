  5. 5 2023

    Which one of the following explains the practice of 'Vattakirutal' as mentioned in Sangam poems?

    1. AKings employing women bodyguards
    2. BLearned persons assembling in royal courts to discuss religious and philosophical matters
    3. CYoung girls keeping watch over agricultural fields and driving away birds and animals

    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. A king defeated in a battle committing ritual suicide by starving himself to death

    Explanation

    'Vattakirutal' in Sangam literature refers to the practice of a defeated king facing towards the north and starving himself to death as a ritual act after losing a battle, reflecting the warrior ethic of the age.

  8. 8 2020

    With reference to the scholars/litterateurs of ancient India, consider the following statements:
    1. Panini is associated with Pushyamitra Shunga.
    2. Amarasimha is associated with Harshavardhana.
    3. Kalidasa is associated with Chandra Gupta –
    II.
    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 and 2 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. 3 only

    Explanation

    Panini, the grammarian, is traditionally placed much earlier than the Shunga period, and Amarasimha (author of Amarakosha) is associated with the Gupta court, not Harshavardhana. Kalidasa's association with the Gupta ruler Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya) is the widely accepted view, so only statement 3 is correct.

    Assertion (A): The Aham and Puram poems of the Padinen Kilukanakku group formed a continuation of the Sangam composition. Reason (R): They were included under the Post-Sangam works as against the Sangam works proper.

    1. ABoth A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. BBoth A and R are true, but R is not a correct explanation of A
    3. CA is true, but R is false
    4. DA is false, but R is true
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation

    The Aham and Puram poems of the Padinen Kilukanakku group are counted among the Post-Sangam works (as opposed to the eighteen major Sangam works proper), yet they carried forward the Sangam poetic conventions — so both the Assertion and the Reason are true, and the Reason correctly explains the Assertion.
