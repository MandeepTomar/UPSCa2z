  1. 1 2025

    The first Gandharva Mahavidyalaya, a music training school, was set up in 1901 by Vishnu Digambar Paluskar in

    1. ADelhi
    2. BGwalior
    3. CUjjain
    4. DLahore
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Lahore

    Explanation

    Vishnu Digambar Paluskar founded the Gandharva Mahavidyalaya in Lahore in 1901. The aim was to teach music to any aspirant without the necessity of being associated with a guru and gharana.

  2. 2 2024

    Which one of the following was the latest inclusion in the Intangible Cultural Heritage List of UNESCO?

    1. AChhau dance
    2. BDurga puja
    3. CGarba dance
    4. DKumbh mela
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Garba dance

    Explanation

    Garba, a devotional folk dance from Gujarat performed during Navaratri to honour feminine energy or 'Shakti', was inscribed in UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage List in December 2023.

  3. 3 2018

    With reference to cultural history of India, consider the following statements:
    1. Most of the Tyagaraja Kritis are devotional songs in praise of Lord Krishna.
    2. Tyagaraja created several new ragas.
    3. Annamacharya and Tyagaraja are contemporaries.
    4. Annamacharya keertanas are devotional songs in praise of Lord Venkateshwara.

    Which of the statements given above are correct?

    1. A1 and 3 only
    2. B2 and 4 only
    3. C1, 2 and 3
    4. D2, 3 and 4
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 and 4 only

    Explanation

    Saint Tyagaraja, an 18th-century Carnatic composer, is known for his devotional Telugu compositions dedicated to Lord Rama (not Krishna). He introduced several new ragas. Annamacharya (15th century) and Tyagaraja (18th century) were not contemporaries.

  9. 9 2014

    With reference to Sattriya, consider the following statements:
    1. It is a combination of music, dance and drama.
    2. It is a centuries-old living tradition of the Vaishnavites of Assam.
    3. It is based on classical Ragas and Talas of devotional songs composed by Tulsidas, Kabir and
    Mirabai. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B1 and 2 only
    3. C2 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 1 and 2 only

    Explanation

    Sattriya is indeed a combination of music, dance, and drama, and is a centuries-old Vaishnavite tradition of Assam founded by Srimanta Sankardeva. However, it is based on devotional compositions by Sankardeva and Madhavdeva, not on songs of Tulsidas, Kabir, and Mirabai, so only statements 1 and 2 are correct.

  11. 11 2014

    2. Bmusical tradition in North-West India
    3. Cclassical vocal music in South India
    4. Dpietra dura tradition in Central India
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. musical tradition in North-West India

  12. 12 2013

    In the context of cultural history of India, a pose in dance and dramatics called ‘Tribhanga’ has been a favourite of Indian artists from ancient times till today. Which one of the following statements best describes this pose?

    1. AOne leg is bent and the body is slightly but oppositely curved at waist and neck
    2. BFacial expressions, hand gestures and make-up are combined to symbolize certain epic or historic characters
    3. CMovements of body, face and hands are used to express oneself or to tell a story
    4. DA little smile, slightly curved waist and certain hand gestures are emphasized to express the feelings of love or eroticism
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. One leg is bent and the body is slightly but oppositely curved at waist and neck

    Explanation

    Tribhanga is a classical dance/sculpture pose in which the body is bent at three points — neck, waist, and knee — creating an S-shaped curve, seen widely in Indian temple sculpture and dance postures.

  13. 13 2012

    With reference to Dhrupad, one of the major traditions of India that has been kept alive for centuries, which of the following statements are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 only

    Explanation

    Dhrupad is one of the oldest surviving forms of Hindustani classical vocal music, characterised by its serious, meditative style and traditionally accompanied by the pakhawaj rather than the tabla.

  14. 14 2012

    How do you distinguish between Kuchipudi and Bharatanatyam dances?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. 1 only

    Explanation

    Kuchipudi (from Andhra Pradesh) traditionally allowed men to play female roles and included spoken dialogue as part of its dance-drama format, distinguishing it from the more solo, expression-focused Bharatanatyam of Tamil Nadu.

  23. 23 2003

    Consider the following statement:

    1. AOnly 1
    2. BOnly 2
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2

    Explanation

    Ibrahim Adil Shah II of Bijapur, a patron of music and the arts, composed the 'Kitab-i-Nauras', devotional songs blending Hindu and Islamic themes. Amir Khusrau, court poet-musician of the Delhi Sultanate, is traditionally credited with originating the Qawwali musical style in India.

  28. 28 2000

    Consider the following statements regarding the Chakiarkoothu form of dance:
    I. It is performed by Chakiar caste.
    II. It cannot be traditionally witnessed by the higher caste Hindus.
    III. Mizhavu is the accompanying instrument.
    IV. Its theatre form is called koothambalam.
    Which of these statements are correct?

    1. AI, III and IV
    2. BI, II and III
    3. CII, III and IV
    4. DI, II and IV
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. I, III and IV

    Explanation

    Chakiarkoothu is a traditional Kerala performance art performed by the Chakiar caste inside the koothambalam (temple theatre), accompanied by the mizhavu drum, and was traditionally restricted from being witnessed by higher-caste Hindus in certain contexts — making statements I, III and IV correct while II relates to caste-access restrictions differently as per the accepted key.
