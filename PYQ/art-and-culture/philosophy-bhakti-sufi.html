  1. 1 2022

    With reference to Indian history, consider the following pairs:
    Historical person : Known as
    1. Aryadeva : Jaina scholar
    2. Dignaga : Buddhist scholar
    3. Nathamuni : Vaishnava scholar

    How many pairs given above are correctly matched?

    1. ANone of the pairs
    2. BOnly one pair
    3. COnly two pairs
    4. DAll three pairs
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Only two pairs

    Explanation

    Aryadeva was a Buddhist scholar and author of Catuhsataka. Dignaga was a Buddhist scholar and founder of Indian logic. Nathamuni was a Vaishnava theologian who compiled the Nalayira Divya Prabandham. Pairs 2 and 3 are correct.

  2. 2 2022

    The world's second tallest statue in sitting pose of Ramanuja was inaugurated by the Prime Minister of India at Hyderabad recently. Which one of the following statements correctly represents the teachings of Ramanuja?

    1. AThe best means of salvation was devotion.
    2. BVedas are eternal, self-existent and wholly authoritative.
    3. CLogical arguments were essential means for the highest bliss.
    4. DSalvation was to be obtained through meditation.
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. The best means of salvation was devotion.

    Explanation

    Option (a) is correct: Ramanuja tried to assimilate bhakti to the tradition of Vedas. He argued that in order to attain salvation, the grace of God was more important than knowledge about him.

  3. 3 2020

    With reference to the cultural history of India, consider the following pairs:
    1. Parivrajaka : Renunciant and Wanderer
    2. Shramana : Priest with a high status
    3. Upasaka : Lay follower of Buddhism

    Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

    1. A1 and 2 only
    2. B1 and 3 only
    3. C2 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 1 and 3 only

    Explanation

    Parivrajaka means roaming ascetic in multiple Indian traditions. Shramana includes non-Vedic traditions like Buddhism and Jainism, not Brahmanical priests. Upasaka/upasika are lay followers. Thus, only pairs 1 and 3 are correct.

  4. 4 2024

    With reference to ancient India, Gautama Buddha was generally known by which of the following epithets?
    1. Nayaputta
    2. Shakyamuni
    3. Tathagata
    Select the correct answer using the code given below:

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C1, 2 and 3
    4. DNone of the above are epithets of Gautama Buddha
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 and 3 only

    Explanation

    Gautama Buddha was known by epithets like Shakyamuni ('sage of the Shakyas') and Tathagata ('one who has thus gone/come'). 'Nayaputta' (Nataputta) was actually a title of Mahavira, the Jain Tirthankara, not the Buddha.

  5. 5 2024

    Consider the following statements:
    1. There are no parables in Upanishads.
    2. Upanishads were composed earlier than the Puranas. Which of the statements given above
    is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 only

    Explanation

    Upanishads do contain illustrative parables and stories used to convey philosophical ideas, making statement 1 incorrect. Upanishads (composed roughly 800–200 BCE) are indeed generally dated earlier than the Puranas, which took final shape later.

  6. 6 2023

    "Souls are not only the property of animal and plant life, but also of rocks, running water and many other natural objects not looked on as living by other religious sects." The above statement reflects one of the core beliefs of which one of the following religious sects of ancient India?

    1. ABuddhism
    2. BJainism
    3. CShaivism
    4. DVaishnavism
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Jainism

    Explanation

    This animistic belief, attributing souls to inanimate natural objects like rocks and water in addition to plants and animals, reflects a core tenet of Jainism, which holds that even elements like earth, water, fire, and air possess souls (jiva).

  7. 7 2022

    With reference to Indian history, consider the following texts:
    1. Nettipakarana
    2. Parishishtaparvan
    3. Avadanashataka
    4. Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana
    Which of the above are Jaina texts?

    1. A1, 2 and 3
    2. B2 and 4 only
    3. C1, 3 and 4
    4. D2, 3 and 4
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 and 4 only

    Explanation

    Parishishtaparvan (by Hemachandra) and Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana (by Jinasena) are Jaina texts. Nettipakarana and Avadanashataka are Buddhist texts, so only 2 and 4 are correctly matched.

  8. 8 2021

    Consider the following statements :
    1. St. Francis Xavier was one of the founding members of the Jesuit Order.
    2. St. Francis Xavier died in Goa and a church is dedicated to him there.
    3. The Feast of St. Francis Xavier is celebrated in Goa each year.
    Which of the statements given above are correct?

    1. A1 and 2 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C1 and 3 only
    4. D1,2 and3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. 1 and 3 only

    Explanation

    St. Francis Xavier was indeed a founding member of the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) and his feast is celebrated in Goa. However, he died in 1552 on Shangchuan Island off the coast of China, not in Goa, though his relics were later brought to Goa's Basilica of Bom Jesus.

  9. 9 2020

    With reference to the cultural history of India, which one of the following is the correct description of the term "paramitas"?

    1. AThe earliest Dharmashastra texts written in aphoristic (sutra) style
    2. BPhilosophical schools that did not accept the authority of Vedas
    3. CPerfections whose attainment led to the Bodhisattva path
    4. DPowerful merchant guilds of early medieval South India
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Perfections whose attainment led to the Bodhisattva path

    Explanation

    'Paramitas' refers to the 'perfections' — such as generosity, morality, and wisdom — whose cultivation and attainment mark the path of a Bodhisattva towards enlightenment in Buddhist thought.

  10. 10 2019

    Consider the following:
    1. Deification of the Buddha
    2. Treading the path of Bodhisattvas
    3. Image worship and rituals
    Which of the above is/are the feature/features of Mahayana Buddhism?

    1. A1 only
    2. B1 and 2 only
    3. C2 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation

    Mahayana Buddhism is marked by the deification of the Buddha, the ideal of following the Bodhisattva path to help all beings attain liberation, and the practice of image worship and elaborate rituals — all three features listed are correct.

  11. 11 2018

    With reference to the religious practices in India, the “Sthanakvasi” sect belongs to

    1. ABuddhism
    2. BJainism
    3. CVaishnavism
    4. DShaivism
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Jainism

    Explanation

    The Sthanakvasi sect is a reformist branch of Jainism that rejects idol worship and instead emphasises meditation in simple prayer halls (sthanaks).

  12. 12 2018

    With reference to Indian history, who among the following is a future Buddha, yet to come to save the world ?

    1. AAvalokiteshvara
    2. BLokesvara
    3. CMaitreya
    4. DPadmapani
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Maitreya

    Explanation

    Maitreya is regarded in Buddhist tradition as the future Buddha who will appear on Earth to teach the pure dharma once the teachings of Gautama Buddha are forgotten.

  13. 13 2016

    With reference to the religious history of India, consider the following statements:
    1. The concept of Bodhisattva is central to Hinayana sect of Buddhism.
    2. Bodhisattva is a compassionate one on his way to enlightenment.
    3. Bodhisattva delays achieving his own salvation to help all sentient beings on their path to it.
    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C2 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 and 3 only

    Explanation

    The concept of Bodhisattva — a compassionate being who delays personal salvation to help others attain enlightenment — is central to Mahayana Buddhism, not Hinayana (Theravada), which focuses more on individual liberation (Arhatship).

  14. 14 2014

    Which one of the following pairs does not form part of the six systems of Indian Philosophy?

    1. AMimamsa and Vedanta
    2. BNyaya and Vaisheshika
    3. CLokayata and Kapalika
    4. DSankhya and Yoga
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Lokayata and Kapalika

    Explanation

    The six orthodox systems (Shad-darshanas) of Indian philosophy are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, and Vedanta. Lokayata (materialist) and Kapalika (a Shaiva Tantric sect) are not among these six systems.

  15. 15 2014

    4. DMundaka Upanishad

  16. 16 2014

    Consider the following statements:
    1. ‘Bijak’ is a composition of the teachings of Saint Dadu Dayal.
    2. The Philosophy of Pushti Marg was propounded by Madhvacharya.
    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Neither 1 nor 2

    Explanation

    'Bijak' is actually a compilation of the teachings and verses of Kabir, not Dadu Dayal, making statement 1 incorrect. The Pushti Marg philosophy was propounded by Vallabhacharya, not Madhvacharya, making statement 2 also incorrect.

  17. 17 2013

    Which one of the following describes best the concept of Nirvana in Buddhism?

    1. AThe extinction of the flame of desire
    2. BThe complete annihilation of self
    3. CA state of bliss and rest
    4. DA mental stage beyond all comprehension
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. The extinction of the flame of desire

    Explanation

    In Buddhist philosophy, Nirvana refers to the extinguishing of the 'flame' of desire, craving, and attachment, leading to the cessation of suffering — rather than annihilation of the self or a state of rest.

  18. 18 2013

    Which of the following statements is/are applicable to Jain doctrine?
    1. The surest way of annihilating Karma is to practice penance.
    2. Every object, even the smallest particle, has a soul.
    3. Karma is the bane of the soul and must be ended.
    Select the correct answer using the codes given below.

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C1 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation

    Jain doctrine holds that penance helps annihilate accumulated karma, that even the smallest particles of matter possess a soul (jiva), and that karma binds and burdens the soul, needing to be shed for liberation — all three statements are correct.

  19. 19 2013

    With reference to the history of philosophical thought in India, consider the following statements regarding Sankhya school :
    1. Sankhya does not accept the theory of rebirth or transmigration of soul.
    2. Sankhya holds that it is the self-knowledge that leads to liberation and not any exterior
    influence or agent. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 only

    Explanation

    The Sankhya school does accept the theory of rebirth and transmigration of the soul (making statement 1 incorrect), but it does hold that liberation comes through self-knowledge (viveka) distinguishing purusha from prakriti, without need for an external divine agent.

  20. 20 2011

    The Jain philosophy holds that the world is created and maintained by

    1. AUniversal Law
    2. BUniversal Truth
    3. CUniversal Faith
    4. DUniversal Soul
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Universal Law

    Explanation

    Jain philosophy holds that the universe is governed by an eternal, uncreated cosmic/moral law rather than by a creator God, reflecting its non-theistic worldview.

  21. 21 2010

    1. ANagarjuna
    2. BTukaram
    3. CTyagaraja
    4. DVallabhacharya

  24. 24 2004

    1. ARig-veda
    2. BYajur-veda
    3. CAtharva-veda
    4. DSama-veda

  25. 25 2004

    Which one of the following sequences indicates the correct chronological order?

    1. AShankaracharya - Ramanuja - Chaitanya
    2. BRamanuja - Shankaracharya - Chaitanya
    3. CRamanuja - Chaitanya - Shankaracharya
    4. DShankaracharya - Chaitanya - Ramanuja
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Shankaracharya - Ramanuja - Chaitanya

    Explanation

    The generally accepted chronological order of these Bhakti/Vedanta philosophers is Shankaracharya (8th century) followed by Ramanuja (11th–12th century) and then Chaitanya (15th–16th century).

  26. 26 2002

    In ancient Indian Buddhist monasteries, a ceremony called Pavarana used to be held. It was the

    1. Aoccasion to elect the Sanghaparinyaka and two speakers, one on Dhamma and the other on Vinaya
    2. Bconfession by monks of their offences committed during their stay in the monasteries during the rainy season
    3. Cceremony of initiation of new person into the Buddhist Sangha in which the head is shaved and when yellow robes are offered
    4. Dgathering of Buddhist monks on the next day to the full-moon day of Ashadha when they take up a fixed abode for the next four months or rainy season
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. confession by monks of their offences committed during their stay in the monasteries during the rainy season

    Explanation

    Pavarana was a ceremony held at the end of the monsoon retreat (Vassa) in which Buddhist monks confessed and sought forgiveness for any offences committed during their stay in the monastery.

  27. 27 1998

    The concept of Eight-fold path forms the theme of

    1. ADipavamsa
    2. BDivyavadana
    3. CMahaparinibbana Sutta
    4. DDharma Chakkra Pravartana Sutta
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Dharma Chakkra Pravartana Sutta

    Explanation

    The Dharma Chakra Pravartana Sutta ('Setting in Motion the Wheel of Dharma') records the Buddha's first sermon at Sarnath, in which he expounded the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.

  28. 28 1998

    Many of the Greeks, Kushanas and Shakas embraced Buddhism rather than Hinduism because

    1. ABuddhism was in the ascendant at that time
    2. BThey had renounced the policy of war and violence
    3. CCaste-ridden Hinduism did not attract them
    4. DBuddhism provided easier access to Indian society
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Buddhism provided easier access to Indian society

    Explanation

    Buddhism's relatively simple ethical framework, lack of rigid caste distinctions, and accessible teachings gave foreign ruling groups like the Greeks, Kushanas, and Shakas easier entry into and acceptance within Indian religious and social life compared to caste-bound Brahmanical Hinduism.

  32. 32 1996

    According to ancient Indian cosmogonic ideas the sequential order of the cycle of four aeons (Yugas) is

    1. ADvapara, Krita, Treta and Kali
    2. BKrita, Dvapara, Treta and Kali
    3. CKrita, Treta, Dvapara and Kali
    4. DTreta, Dvapara, Kali and Krita
    Reveal answer

  33. 33 1996

    Which one of the following is not a part of early Jaina literature?

    1. ATherigatha
    2. BAcarangasutra
    3. CSutrakritanga
    4. DBrihatkalpasutra
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Therigatha

    Explanation

  34. 34 1996

    Which of the following were common to both Buddhism and Jainism?
    I. Avoidance of extremities of penance and enjoyment.
    II. Indifference to the authority of the Vedas
    III. Denial of efficacy of rituals
    IV. Non-injury to animal life
    Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

    1. AI, II, III and IV
    2. BII, III and IV
    3. CI, III and IV
    4. DI and II
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. II, III and IV

    Explanation

    Both Buddhism and Jainism arose as heterodox (Shramanic) traditions that showed indifference to Vedic authority, denied the efficacy of ritual sacrifice, and emphasised non-violence towards animal life, though their approach to penance/enjoyment differed somewhat, giving statements II, III and IV as correct.

  38. 38 1995

    According to the Mimamsa system of philosophy, liberation is possible by means of

    1. AJnana
    2. BBhakti
    3. CYoga
    4. DKarma
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Karma

    Explanation

    The Mimamsa school of philosophy holds that liberation (moksha) is achieved primarily through the correct performance of Vedic rituals and duties (karma), in contrast to schools emphasising knowledge (jnana) or devotion (bhakti).
