  1. 1 2022

    With reference to Indian history, who of the following were known as "Kulah-Daran"?

    1. AArab merchants
    2. BQalandars
    3. CPersian calligraphists
    4. DSayyids
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Sayyids

    Explanation

    Option (d) is correct: The Sayyids claim descent from the Prophet through his daughter Fatima. The Sayyid put on a special pointed cap (Kulah) and they were known as Kulah-Daran.

  2. 2 2021

    Consider the following pairs:
    (Historical place) : (Well-known for)
    1. Burzahom : Rock-cut shrines
    2. Chandra-ketugarh : Terracotta art
    3. Ganeshwar : Copper artifacts

    Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

    1. A1 only
    2. B1 and 2
    3. C3 only
    4. D2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. 2 and 3

    Explanation

    Pair 1 is not correctly matched: Burzahom, in Kashmir Valley, is a Neolithic site known for underground pit dwellings. Pair 2 is correctly matched: Chandraketugarh in West Bengal is famous for terracotta art. Pair 3 is correctly matched: Ganeshwar produced many copper tools and ornaments.

  5. 5 2014

    Chaitra 1 of the national calendar based on the Saka Era corresponds to which one of the following dates of the Gregorian calendar in a normal year of 365 days?

    1. A22nd March (or 21st March)
    2. B15th May (or 16th May)
    3. C31st March (or 30th March)
    4. D21st April (or 20th April)
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. 22nd March (or 21st March)

    Explanation

    Chaitra 1 of the Saka-era National Calendar corresponds to 22 March in a normal year (21 March in a leap year) of the Gregorian calendar.

  6. 6 2013

    Consider the following pairs: Tribe State
    1. Limboo (Limbu) : Sikkim
    2. Karbi : Himachal Pradesh
    3. Dongaria Kondh : Odisha
    4. Bonda : Tamil Nadu
    Which of the above pairs are correctly matched?

    1. A1 and 3 only
    2. B2 and 4 only
    3. C1, 3 and 4 only
    4. D1, 2, 3 and 4
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. 1 and 3 only

    Explanation

    Limboo/Limbu is correctly associated with Sikkim, and Dongaria Kondh with Odisha. However, Karbi is a tribe of Assam (not Himachal Pradesh) and Bonda is a tribe of Odisha (not Tamil Nadu), so only pairs 1 and 3 are correct.

  11. 11 2008

    Consider the following statements:
    1. Orange Prize is awarded to a work of published fiction in English by a woman.
    2. Pulitzer Prize is awarded by the Commonwealth Foundation to a citizen of any Commonwealth
    country for any literary work in English. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. A1 only
    2. B2 only
    3. CBoth 1 and 2
    4. DNeither 1 nor 2
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. 1 only

    Explanation

    The Orange Prize (now the Women's Prize for Fiction) is correctly awarded to a woman author for published fiction in English. The Pulitzer Prize, however, is awarded by Columbia University to American citizens/works, not by the Commonwealth Foundation to any Commonwealth citizen, making only statement 1 correct.
