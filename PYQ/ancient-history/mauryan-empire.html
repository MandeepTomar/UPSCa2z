Mauryan Empire UPSC PYQ — Chandragupta, Ashoka & Arthashastra

  1. 1 2025

    Pradeshika, Rajuka and Yukta were important officers at which level?

    1. AVillage level
    2. BDistrict level
    3. CProvincial level
    4. DCentral level
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. District level

    Explanation

    These officers were listed at the district level; Pradeshika was in charge, Rajuka handled district duties and Yukta assisted in administration.

  2. 2 2025

    Which pairs are correctly matched:
    1. Asmaka – Godavari
    2. Kamboja – Vipas
    3. Avanti – Mahanadi
    4. Kosala – Sarayu

    1. AOnly one
    2. BOnly two
    3. COnly three
    4. DAll four
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Only two

    Explanation

    Asmaka–Godavari and Kosala–Sarayu are correct; Kamboja–Vipas and Avanti–Mahanadi are not correct.

  3. 3 2022

    According to Kautilya's Arthashastra, which statements are correct:
    1. A person could become a slave as a result of judicial punishment.
    2. A female slave became free if she bore her master a son.
    3. A son born to a female slave and fathered by her master got the status of the master's son.

    1. A1 and 2 only
    2. B2 and 3 only
    3. C1 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. 2 and 3 only

    Explanation

    Enslavement was mainly through self-sale or capture, not judicial punishment. It also says the master's child by a female slave could be legally recognised, and the mother was considered free.

  4. 4 1998

    Assertion (A): According to Asoka's edicts social harmony among the people was more important than religious devotion.
    Reason (R): He spread ideas of equity instead of promotion of religion.

    1. ABoth A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. BBoth A and R are true, but R is NOT a correct explanation of A
    3. CA is true, but R is false
    4. DA is false, but R is true
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation

    Asoka's Dhamma emphasized ethical conduct, tolerance, and social harmony over sectarian religious practices. He advocated equity, moral values, and respect across communities rather than promoting Buddhism exclusively. The Reason correctly explains the Assertion.

  5. 5 1998

    Which one of the following ancient Indian records is the earliest royal order to preserve foodgrains to be utilised during the crises in the country?

    1. ASohagaura Copper-plate
    2. BRummindei pillar-edict of Asoka
    3. CPrayaga-Prasasti
    4. DMehrauli Pillar inscription of Chandra
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Sohagaura Copper-plate

    Explanation

    The Sohagaura copper-plate is considered the earliest known royal directive related to food security in ancient India. It mentions kosthagara (granaries) established at strategic junctions for distribution during crises.

  6. 6 1998

    The Asokan major rock edicts which tell us about the Sangam Kingdom include rock edicts

    1. AI and X
    2. BI and XI
    3. CII and XIII
    4. DII and XIV
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. II and XIII

    Explanation

    Major Rock Edict II mentions southern kingdoms such as Cholas, Pandyas, Satyaputras, and Keralaputras. Major Rock Edict XIII also mentions Dhamma influence reaching southern kingdoms including Cholas and Pandyas.

  7. 7 1997

    Which one of the following statements regarding Asokan stone pillars is incorrect?

    1. AThese are highly polished
    2. BThese are monolithic
    3. CThe shaft of pillars is tapering in shape
    4. DThese are parts of architectural structures
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. These are parts of architectural structures

    Explanation

    Asokan pillars were freestanding commemorative monuments placed at important sites, not structural supports integrated into architectural frameworks. They were monolithic, highly polished, and had tapering shafts.

  8. 8 1997

    Which one of the following edicts mentions the personal name of Asoka?

    1. AKalsi
    2. BRummindei
    3. CSpecial Kalinga Edict
    4. DMaski
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Maski

    Explanation

    The Maski Edict is the first inscription where Ashoka's personal name is explicitly mentioned as 'Ashoka', resolving earlier scholarly debates over titles like Devanampriya Priyadarsi.

  9. 9 1996

    Which one of the following texts of ancient India allows divorce to a wife deserted by her husband?

    1. AKamasutra
    2. BManavadharmashastra
    3. CSukra Nitisara
    4. DArthashastra
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Arthashastra

    Explanation

    Arthashastra, authored by Kautilya, provided legal provisions for divorce, unlike religious law books. It allowed divorce for a wife deserted by her husband, reflecting a pragmatic and legalistic approach.

  10. 10 1995

    The name by which Asoka is generally referred to in his inscriptions is

    1. AChakravarti
    2. BDharmadeva
    3. CDharmakirti
    4. DPriyadarsi
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. Priyadarsi

    Explanation

    In most of his inscriptions, Emperor Asoka refers to himself as Devanampriya ('Beloved of the Gods') and Priyadarsi ('He who looks with kindness').

  11. 11 1993

    Chanakya was also known as

    1. ABhattasvamin
    2. BRajasekhara
    3. CVishnugupta
    4. DVisakhadatta
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Vishnugupta

    Explanation

    Chanakya is traditionally identified with Kautilya and Vishnugupta — both names appear in his work Arthashastra. He played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Maurya Empire.

  12. 12 1992

    In ancient India the territorial name Tosali referred to

    1. AThe region between the rivers Jhelum and Chenab
    2. BThe basin of the river Son
    3. CThe delta of the river Cauvery
    4. DThe area of the river Mahanadi
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: D. The area of the river Mahanadi

    Explanation

    Tosali was a prominent political and cultural region in ancient Odisha (Kalinga). The River Mahanadi served as the dividing line between Uttara Tosali and Dakshina Tosali.

  13. 13 2022

    Consider the following pairs:
    Site of Ashoka's major rock edicts : Location in the State of
    1. Dhauli : Odisha
    2. Erragudi : Andhra Pradesh
    3. Jaugada : Madhya Pradesh
    4. Kalsi : Karnataka
    How many pairs given above are correctly matched?

    1. AOnly one pair
    2. BOnly two pairs
    3. COnly three pairs
    4. DAll four pairs
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. Only two pairs

    Explanation

    Dhauli (Odisha) and Erragudi (Andhra Pradesh) are correctly matched. Jaugada is also in Odisha, not Madhya Pradesh, and Kalsi is in Uttarakhand, not Karnataka.

  14. 14 2020

    Which one of the following rulers advised his subjects through an inscription thus: "Whosoever praises his religious sect or blames other sects out of excessive devotion to his own sect, he rather injures his own sect very severely."

    1. AAshoka
    2. BSamudragupta
    3. CHarshavardhana
    4. DKrishnadeva Raya
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Ashoka

    Explanation

    This is a paraphrase of Ashoka's Rock Edict XII, which calls for restraint in speech regarding other religious sects and promotes toleration among different faiths.

  15. 15 2016

    Who among the following had first deciphered the edicts of Emperor Ashoka?

    1. AGeorg Bühler
    2. BJames Prinsep
    3. CMax Müller
    4. DWilliam Jones
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: B. James Prinsep

    Explanation

    James Prinsep, an official of the East India Company, deciphered the Brahmi script in 1837, enabling the reading of Ashokan edicts and identifying the king referred to as 'Devanampiya Piyadasi' as Ashoka.

  16. 16 2000

    Assertion (A): Ashoka annexed Kalinga to the Mauryan Empire.
    Reason (R): Kalinga controlled the land and sea routes to South India.

    1. ABoth A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. BBoth A and R are true, but R is not a correct explanation of A
    3. CA is true, but R is false
    4. DA is false, but R is true
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation

    Ashoka did annex Kalinga after the famous war of 261 BCE, and Kalinga's control over key land and sea trade routes to the south was a major strategic reason for the conquest.
