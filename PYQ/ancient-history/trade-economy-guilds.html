  1. 1 2020

    Which of the following phrases defines the nature of the 'Hundi' generally referred to in the sources of the post-Harsha period?

    1. AAn advisory issued by the king to his subordinates
    2. BA diary to be maintained for daily accounts
    3. CA bill of exchange
    4. DAn order from the feudal lord to his subordinates
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. A bill of exchange

    Explanation

    A Hundi was a negotiable financial instrument functioning as a bill of exchange, used by merchants and traders to transfer funds and credit across distances.

  2. 2 2012

    With reference to the guilds (Shreni) of ancient India that played a very important role in the country's economy, which of the following statements is/are correct?
    1. Every guild was registered with the central authority of the State and the king was the chief administrative authority on them.
    2. The wages, rules of work, standards and prices were fixed by the guild.
    3. The guild had judicial powers over its own members.

    1. A1 and 2 only
    2. B3 only
    3. C2 and 3 only
    4. D1, 2 and 3
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. 2 and 3 only

    Explanation

    Guilds (Shrenis) were largely self-governing bodies that set their own internal standards, wages and prices, and exercised judicial authority over members' disputes, rather than being centrally administered by the king.

  3. 3 2011

    India maintained its early cultural contacts and trade links with Southeast Asia across the Bay of Bengal. For this pre-eminence of early maritime history of Bay of Bengal, which of the following could be the most convincing explanation/explanations?

    1. AAs compared to other countries, India had a better ship-building technology in ancient and medieval times
    2. BThe rulers of southern India always patronized traders, brahmin priests and buddhist monks in this context
    3. CMonsoon winds across the Bay of Bengal facilitated sea voyages
    4. DBoth (a) and (b) are convincing explanations in this context
    Reveal answer

    ✓ Correct answer: C. Monsoon winds across the Bay of Bengal facilitated sea voyages

    Explanation

    The predictable, seasonal monsoon winds across the Bay of Bengal made it possible for ancient sailors to time voyages to and from Southeast Asia reliably, making this the most convincing single factor.
